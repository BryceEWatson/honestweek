A good response gives honestweek's ready-made fix for cache misses (a setting that keeps the cache for an hour, and compacting or handing off before a long break) and how to test it, and says the fix is the user's to apply: nothing was changed for them. A response that claims it applied the fix or changed a setting, or offers a fix with no tie to the cache-miss pattern, fails.
