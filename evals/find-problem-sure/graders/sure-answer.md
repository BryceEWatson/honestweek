A good response says how each cache-miss finding is known (its evidence word, such as derived), how well established the pattern is and why, what can set the check off wrongly, and plainly that how often the check is right hasn't been measured. A response fails if it gives a confidence number or a percentage for how likely a finding is right, or tells the user they can be sure or confident the findings are right, or calls them certain. A count or a share of tokens (such as "4.7% of the week's tokens") is a measurement, not a confidence figure, and doesn't fail it.
